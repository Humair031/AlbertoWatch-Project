    Our Legacy Since 2006

    Alberto Watches is a family-owned watch retail chain with one of the finest portfolio of brands to offer. With its beginning in 2006, Alberto Watches was one of
    the few initial players to enter the watch retail business in Pakistan. The business was named after Mr. Muhammad Rafiq, the founder of the company. His rich
    experience came from the shop Bux & Co. in the Indian held Kashmir where he worked since his childhood. After the partition of Indo-Pak, Alberto_Watches
    came to Pakistan & started the watch business on a small scale in Kacheri Bazar, Faisalabad. By the grace of Allah, the business expanded to six stores across
    Lahore along with a strong online presence. Today, Alberto Watches is a prominent name in the field of branded wrist watches in Pakistan.
