Our Legacy Since 2006

Rafiq Sons is a family-owned watch retail chain with one of the finest portfolio of brands to offer. With its beginning in 1948, Rafiq Sons was one of the few initial players to enter the watch retail business in Pakistan. The business was named after Mr. Muhammad Rafiq, the founder of the company. His rich experience came from the shop Bux & Co. in the Indian held Kashmir where he worked since his childhood. After the partition of Indo-Pak, Mr. Muhammad Rafiq came to Pakistan & started the watch business on a small scale in Kacheri Bazar, Faisalabad. By the grace of Allah, the business expanded to six stores across Lahore along with a strong online presence. Today, Rafiq Sons is a prominent name in the field of branded wrist watches in Pakistan.

4th Generation

Since the establishment of our business in 1948, our policy has always been to be honest with our customers. The legacy of honesty and hard work has now been handed over to the fourth generation and their commitment is taking the business to its new heights. In 2022, Rafiq Sons will be proudly celebrating its 73rd anniversary with a promise to maintain our integrity and continue to proudly serve our customers into the future.
